**Chanda Jha** (b. 1831, Pindaruch in Darbhanga), known as *Kavi Chandra*, was the first poet to render the **Ramayana into Maithili** — the *Mithila Bhasha Ramayana* — and in doing so helped lift Maithili into a fully literary language. It is often said that what **Vidyapati** did for Maithili in the fourteenth century, Chanda Jha did for it in the nineteenth.

Beyond the Ramayana his pen ranged across the *Lakshmishwar Vilas*, *Geet-Sudha*, *Padyavali* and the play *Ahalya-Charit Natak*. His work stands as the bridge between Mithila's classical and modern literary ages.
