**Nagarjun** — the pen name of **Baidyanath Mishra** (1911–1998), also known by his Maithili nom de plume *Yatri* — was the great "People's Poet" (*Jankavi*) of his age, writing with equal power in **Maithili** and Hindi. His verse spoke for the farmer, the poor and the oppressed, and twice landed him in jail.

A restless wanderer and a fierce social conscience, he won the **Sahitya Akademi Award** (1969) and stands at the head of modern Maithili poetry.
