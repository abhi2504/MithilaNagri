**Sushant Singh Rajput** (1986–2020), born in **Purnia** in the Mithila region, rose from popular television to a celebrated Hindi-film career with performances in *Kai Po Che!*, *M.S. Dhoni: The Untold Story* and *Chhichhore*. Curious, self-taught and widely admired, he became one of his generation's most followed actors before his untimely death in 2020.

For many in Bihar and Mithila he remains a figure of pride and affection — a small-town boy whose talent carried him to the front rank of Indian cinema.
