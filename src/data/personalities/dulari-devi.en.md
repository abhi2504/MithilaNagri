**Dulari Devi** (b. 1968), born into a marginalised Mallah family in **Ranti**, learned Madhubani painting while working as a domestic helper in the home of the master artist Mahasundari Devi. From those beginnings she rose to national honour, painting the life of her community — and even the COVID lockdown — in the Mithila idiom.

Awarded the **Padma Shri (2021)**, with work collected by Princeton University, she embodies the way Mithila's art has lifted its women across the lines of caste and class.
