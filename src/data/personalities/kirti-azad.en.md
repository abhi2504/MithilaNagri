**Kirti Azad** (b. 1959), son of the Maithil Chief Minister **Bhagwat Jha Azad**, was an all-rounder in the Indian side that won the **1983 Cricket World Cup** — his bowling famously containing Ian Botham in the Lord's semi-final. A right-hand batsman and off-spinner, he played Tests and one-day internationals through the 1980s.

In public life he carried the family's political mantle, winning the **Darbhanga** Lok Sabha seat in 2014. Outspoken to a fault, he remains one of the most recognisable Maithil faces in Indian sport and politics alike.
