**Neetu Chandra** (b. 1984), an actor across Hindi and southern cinema, is — with her brother **Nitin Chandra** — a driving force behind the revival of **Maithili film**. Through their Champaran Talkies, they produced **Mithila Makhaan**, the first Maithili film to win a **National Film Award** (2016), and worked to build platforms for regional cinema from the Mithila belt.

A trained Bharatanatyam dancer and martial artist, she has used her national profile to push Maithili stories onto a bigger stage.
