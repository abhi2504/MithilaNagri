**Harimohan Jha** (1908–1984) is the great modern prose-writer of Maithili — sometimes called its "Vidyapati of prose". His novel **Kanyadan** and its sequel, and above all the gently devastating satire of **Khattar Kakak Tarang**, used wit and warmth to question the rigidities of Maithil Brahmin society.

Widely read and loved, his books brought a modern, self-aware sensibility to Maithili literature. He received the **Sahitya Akademi Award** posthumously for his autobiography.
