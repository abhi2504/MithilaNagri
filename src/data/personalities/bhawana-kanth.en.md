**Bhawana Kanth** (b. 1992), from **Darbhanga**, is one of the first three women inducted as **fighter pilots** in the Indian Air Force, and among the first qualified for combat missions. Flying the MiG-21, she became a symbol of changing horizons for young women across the region.

In a land long proud of its scholars and poets, she represents a new kind of Mithila achiever — and an inspiration to a generation of girls in Bihar's towns and villages.
