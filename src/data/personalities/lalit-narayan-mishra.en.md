**Lalit Narayan Mishra** (1923–1975), a freedom fighter and Union minister, was among Mithila's most prominent national politicians. As **Railway Minister** he pushed the expansion of the rail network into the region, and he championed Maithil causes in Delhi. He died in a bomb blast at **Samastipur** in 1975 while inaugurating a new broad-gauge line.

His name endures across Mithila — most visibly in the **Lalit Narayan Mithila University** at Darbhanga and the institutions that bear his memory.
