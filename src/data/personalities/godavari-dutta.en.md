**Godavari Dutta** (1930–2024), who made her life in **Ranti**, was a supreme exponent of the fine-line **Katchni** style of Madhubani painting, working — unusually — with a bamboo stick to draw her shimmering networks of line. Through hardship she built a long career and taught tens of thousands of students.

She was closely linked to the **Mithila Museum** in Tokamachi, Japan, working there for years. Honoured with the **Padma Shri (2019)** at the age of 93, she was one of the last living masters of the classical Mithila tradition.
