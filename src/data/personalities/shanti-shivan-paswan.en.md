**Shanti Devi Paswan and her husband Shivan Paswan** are the great revivalists of the **Godna** style of Madhubani — the tattoo-derived art of Mithila's Dalit communities. Overcoming deep social barriers, they have exhibited across the world and trained tens of thousands of artists, lifting an once-marginalised form to honour.

Their joint **Padma Shri (2024)** recognised both their art and their role in widening who Mithila painting belongs to.
