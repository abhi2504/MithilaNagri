**Surendra Jha 'Suman'** (1910–2002), born at **Ballipur** in Samastipur, was a rare all-rounder of the Maithili renaissance — poet, editor, publisher and elected politician. Head of the Maithili department at Lalit Narayan Mithila University, Darbhanga, and author of some forty books, he won the **Sahitya Akademi Award in 1971** for ***Payasvini***, a collection of lyric poems on the beauty of nature and the simplicity of rural Mithila; he won the Akademi's translation prize again in 1995 for rendering Tagore's plays into Maithili.

In 1948 he founded the **Mithila Press**, dedicating it to spreading awareness of Maithili language, culture and literature — an institution still run by his family. He also served in public life as a member of the **Bihar Legislative Assembly** and as **Member of the Lok Sabha** from Darbhanga (1977–1979).

His career bridges literature and public service: he helped win Maithili its place in the **Sahitya Akademi** and built the printing and editorial infrastructure that allowed a vernacular literature to circulate widely in twentieth-century Mithila.
