**Udit Narayan Jha** (b. 1955), born to a Maithil family on the Bihar–Nepal border, is one of the most successful playback singers in Indian film history. The son of a folk singer, he broke through with *Qayamat Se Qayamat Tak* (1988) and went on to sing thousands of songs across more than three dozen languages, winning multiple **National Film Awards** and Filmfare Awards.

For all his Bollywood fame, he has never let go of his roots — recording in **Maithili** and Bhojpuri and lending his voice to the music of Mithila. With the **Padma Shri** and **Padma Bhushan** to his name, he stands among the region's proudest exports to the wider world of Indian music.
