**Kadhi-bari** is comfort in a bowl — fried **gram-flour dumplings** (*bari*) simmered in a gently soured gravy of yogurt thickened with besan, tempered with mustard seeds and chilli. Ladled over hot rice, it is a staple of the everyday Maithil meal.

*A simple method:* whisk yogurt and besan smooth; bring to a simmer with turmeric, salt and a mustard-seed tempering, stirring so it doesn't split; meanwhile fry small besan dumplings until golden and fold them in to soak up the tangy gravy. Finish with a final tempering of red chilli in ghee.
