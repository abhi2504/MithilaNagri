**Kirtaniya** is the traditional musical theatre of Mithila — a form of sung, dramatised devotion that grew from the *kirtan* and the lyric songs of **Vidyapati** and his successors. Performers enact episodes of Radha and Krishna and the gods, weaving verse, melody and dialogue into long night-time performances.

One of the oldest theatrical traditions of the eastern region, Kirtaniya shows how deeply Mithila's literature and music are entwined — the poet's song becoming, on the village stage, a living drama.
