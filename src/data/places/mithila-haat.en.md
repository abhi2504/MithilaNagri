Opened on **11 January 2023** at Araria Sangram village near Jhanjharpur, **Mithila Haat** is a 26-acre theme-park built to gather Mithila's living culture in one place. A craft bazaar sells **Madhubani paintings**, **Sikki** grasswork and Khadi textiles; an amphitheatre stages Maithil music and dance; and a food court serves regional dishes from *thekua* to makhana sweets.

With boating, a resort and gardens around a central pond, the Haat is designed for families and for travellers beginning a Mithila itinerary. It is the first dedicated cultural-tourism complex for the region, offering artisans a marketplace and visitors an accessible introduction to Maithil heritage.
