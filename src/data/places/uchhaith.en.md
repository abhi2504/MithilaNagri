**Uchhaith**, near Benipatti in Madhubani, is an ancient **Bhagwati (Durga)** shrine on the bank of the Thumne river — a *Siddhapith* of tantric significance. Local tradition holds that it was here that the dull boy who would become **Kalidasa**, India's greatest Sanskrit poet, was blessed by the goddess and awoke to genius.

Especially alive during **Navratri**, Uchhaith joins the region's many Shakti shrines that bind Mithila to the worship of the Devi.
