At **Sonepur**, where the Gandak meets the Ganga on Mithila's western edge, stands the **Hariharnath** temple — uniting **Hari** (Vishnu) and **Hara** (Shiva) in one shrine, traditionally said to have been founded by Rama. Around it gathers the **Sonepur Mela**, once the largest cattle fair in Asia, held at Kartik Purnima.

Pilgrimage, river-confluence and great fair together make Sonepur one of the storied gathering-places of the region.
