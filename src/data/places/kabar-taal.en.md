**Kabar Taal**, near Begusarai, is the largest freshwater **oxbow lake** in Asia — a great crescent left behind by the wandering Gandak. Recognised as a **Ramsar wetland** of international importance in 2020, it hosts hundreds of plant and animal species and is a winter haven for migratory birds along the Central Asian flyway.

In a region defined by its rivers, Kabar Taal is the natural jewel of Mithila — a reminder that the Maithil landscape is as much water and wing as it is temple and field.
