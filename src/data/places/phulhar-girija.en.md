At **Phulhar**, near the Nepal border, stands the **Girija Sthan** — the temple of the goddess Girija (Parvati) where, in the Ramayana, Sita came each day to worship and first beheld **Rama** as he gathered flowers in the royal garden of *Pushpavatika*. A sacred pond and the *Pratham Milan* (first meeting) shrine mark the spot.

A station on the **Mithila Madhya Parikrama**, Phulhar is one of the tender, intimate sites of Sita's story, drawing pilgrims who retrace the geography of the epic.
