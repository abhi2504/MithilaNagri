**Jhijhiya** is the luminous folk dance of Mithila's Dussehra. During Navratri, women and girls balance **earthen pots pierced with many holes** and lit with lamps inside on their heads, dancing in circles by night to devotional songs for the goddess Durga and against ill-will.

Equal parts worship, performance and community ritual — and demanding real poise — Jhijhiya is one of the most beautiful sights of the Maithil autumn.
