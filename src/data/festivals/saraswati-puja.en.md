**Saraswati Puja**, on **Vasant Panchami**, welcomes the spring with the worship of **Saraswati**, goddess of learning, music and the arts. In a land that prizes scholarship above almost all else, it is a festival especially of students and schools, who install and adore her image, dress in yellow, and seek her blessing on their books and instruments.

Across Mithila's towns and villages the day fills with bright pandals and song — a fitting celebration in the home of Vidyapati and the Nyaya philosophers.
