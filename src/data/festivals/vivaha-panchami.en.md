**Vivaha Panchami** marks the marriage of **Rama and Sita**, and nowhere is it kept like at **Janakpur** — Sita's own city — where the wedding is re-enacted each winter amid processions, music and immense crowds drawn from both India and Nepal. The rites recall the whole sequence of the divine wedding, from the *Matkor* gathering of sacred soil to the *vivah* itself.

For Mithila, whose identity is bound up with Sita, Vivaha Panchami is the festival that most fully celebrates the region's place at the heart of the Ramayana.
