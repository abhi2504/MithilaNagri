In Mithila, Holi is **Phaguaa** — and it is as much a music festival as a festival of colour. The eve is marked by **Holika Dahan**, the lighting of a great bonfire that burns away the old year's evil; the next day brings the throwing of *abeer* and coloured water, and above all the singing of **jogira** and *phaguaa* songs in chorus, often laced with cheat and comedy, accompanied by *dholak* and *jhaal*.

The Maithil Phaguaa table is famous in its own right: **malpua** soaked in syrup, *thekua* and, in many households, a robust **mutton curry** — the rare festival on which the region's love of rich, celebratory food is given full rein. It falls in *Phalguna*, the month that gives the festival its Mithila name.
