**Makar Sankranti** — known in Mithila as *Tila Sakrait* — falls in mid-January as the sun turns north, and is kept as a harvest and winter festival of warming foods. Households gather over heaped plates of **dahi-chura** (beaten rice with curd and jaggery) and the sesame-jaggery sweets **tilkut** and *tilwa* that give the day its name.

A festival of bathing, charity and family feasting, it threads the bond between Mithila's people and the rhythm of their fields and seasons.
