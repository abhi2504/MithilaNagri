**Vat Savitri** is the great vrat of married women, kept in the summer month of Jyeshtha. Recalling the legend of **Savitri**, who won her husband Satyavan back from death itself, women fast and wind sacred thread around the **banyan** (*vat*) tree, circling it in prayer for the long life and wellbeing of their husbands.

In Mithila, as across the region, it is one of the warmest expressions of conjugal devotion in the festival year.
