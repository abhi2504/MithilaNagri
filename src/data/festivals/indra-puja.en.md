**Indra Puja** — the worship of **Indra**, the Vedic god of rain and storm, for timely rain and a good harvest — has all but vanished elsewhere in India, yet survives in Mithila. It is a striking link to the region's deep Vedic past, when Indra was among the foremost of gods.

In an agrarian land at the mercy of the monsoon and its rivers, the festival's logic is plain: it is a prayer, from a farming people, to the lord of the rains.
