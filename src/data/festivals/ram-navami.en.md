**Ram Navami**, the ninth day of bright *Chaitra*, marks the birth of **Rama** — and nowhere is it kept with more feeling than in Mithila, the land of his bride **Sita**. Temples across the region hold day-long recitation of the *Ramcharitmanas* and the Ramayana, with *bhajan* and the swinging of the infant Rama's cradle.

The festival has a particular resonance at **Janakpur** and at Sita's birthplace **Punaura Dham** near Sitamarhi, where the bond between the houses of Janaka and Dasharatha is felt as local family history rather than distant epic. It opens the spring festival season that the region's calendar of Sita-centred rites — Sita Navami, Vivaha Panchami — will carry through the year.
