**Naag Panchami** falls in the heart of the monsoon, when the flooded fields of Mithila bring snakes close to human life — and the festival turns that danger into reverence, honouring the **serpents** and the snake-goddess **Manasa** with offerings of milk and prayer for protection.

It belongs to the same watery, monsoon world as Madhushravani and its Bihula–Lakhindar legend — a reminder of how closely Maithil faith is woven into the seasons of river and rain.
