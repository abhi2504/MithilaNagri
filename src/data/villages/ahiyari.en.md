**Ahiyari** is a village in the **Jale** block of **Darbhanga** district, Bihar, close to **Kamtaul** railway station. It is the site of **Ahalya Sthan**, the temple traditionally identified with the *Ramayana* episode in which **Ahalya** — wife of the sage Gautama, cursed to become stone — is released from the curse by the touch of Lord Rama. The site is counted among the earliest Ram-Janaki temples of the region.

The present temple is said to have been built in the **17th century** under the Khandavala (Darbhanga Raj) rulers Maharaja Chhatra Singh and Maharaja Rudra Singh. It stands within the old Gautam-ashram tradition of Mithila and is closely tied to the Sita–Rama legends that pervade the area around Janakpur and Sitamarhi.

Large fairs gather here every year at **Ram Navami** and at **Vivah Panchami**, when Mithila celebrates the marriage of Rama and Sita. The everyday language is **Maithili**, and the shrine is a long-standing stop on the Ramayana pilgrim circuit of north Bihar.
