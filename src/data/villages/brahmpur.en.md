**Brahmpur**, in Darbhanga's Jale block, is associated with the **Gautam Ashram** — the legendary hermitage of the sage Gautama, linked to the Nyaya Sutras and to the tradition of logic for which Mithila became famous across India. The site recalls the region's identity as a land of debate, learning and philosophy.
