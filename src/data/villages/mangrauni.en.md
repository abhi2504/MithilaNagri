**Mangrauni** — whose older name *Mangalwani* is read as "the place of blessings" — is a large Maithil Brahmin village in the **Rajnagar** block of **Madhubani** district, about 2 km from Madhubani town. From early times it has been known for **devotion, temples and astrology**, and counts among the cluster of learned villages around the district headquarters.

Its best-known shrines are the **Budhi Mata (Maa Bhagwati) temple**, revered across Madhubani, and the **Baba Ekadash Rudra Mandir** built in 1953, whose eleven Shivalingas represent the eleven forms of Shiva. It is also remembered as the home village of **Maharani Kamsundari Devi**, the last queen of Raj Darbhanga.

The everyday language is **Maithili**, and the village sits squarely in the Mithila-painting heartland that surrounds Madhubani town, with a long tradition of Sanskrit and *jyotish* (astrology) learning.
