**Dhalkebar**, in Dhanusha district, is the road gateway to **Janakpur** — the point on Nepal's east–west **Mahendra Highway** where travellers turn south for Sita's city. That crossroads role has made it a busy ribbon of transport, trade and bus traffic.

It is also a name known to power engineers on both sides of the border: the **Dhalkebar substation** anchors the high-voltage **Dhalkebar–Muzaffarpur transmission line**, the artery that carries electricity between India and Nepal — a very modern expression of the old Mithila link across the frontier.
