**Lohna** is a village in the **Jhanjharpur** block of **Madhubani** district, Bihar, divided administratively into **Lohna North** and **Lohna South** panchayats. The river **Lakshmana (Lakhandei)** cuts the village into two halves, and **Lohna Road** railway station lies about 2 km away. The **2011 Census** recorded a population of around **7,000** across roughly 1,670 households.

Lohna is remembered as the birthplace of **Umapati Upadhyaya**, the medieval Maithili poet and dramatist whose play *Parijata-harana* — on the quarrel of Krishna's queens Rukmini and Satyabhama over the *parijata* flower — is a landmark of early Maithili theatre, and who was also a scholar of *Nyaya* logic. Local tradition holds that a Sanskrit college still functions on the ruins of an old seat of learning once known as the "University of Lohana."

The village's chief shrine is the **Videshwarnath Mahadev temple**, with an adjoining Parvati temple. The everyday language is **Maithili**, and Lohna ranks among the literary villages of the Kamala–Balan plain in eastern Mithila.
