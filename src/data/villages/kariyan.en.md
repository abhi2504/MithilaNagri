**Kariyan** (also Karian) is a village and gram panchayat in the **Shivaji Nagar** block of **Samastipur** district, Bihar. By local tradition it is the birthplace of **Udayanacharya** (Udayana), the great Maithil Brahmin logician of the *Nyaya* school, author of the *Nyayakusumanjali* and *Atmatattvaviveka* — works credited, alongside those of Kumarila Bhatta, with the philosophical decline of Buddhism in eastern India.

The modern village sits on a **mound roughly twenty feet high** covering about 96 acres; excavations there have yielded antiquities ranging from the **2nd century BCE to the post-1200 CE period**, marking it as a very old centre of habitation. The site lies about 16 km north-east of **Rosera Ghat** railway station and some 28 km from the Samastipur district headquarters.

The village also remembers the Vaishnava saint **Sanaknandanacharya** and the modern Maithili poet **Kali Kant Jha 'Buch.'** The everyday language is **Maithili**, and Kariyan stands as one of Mithila's storied scholar-villages in the tract south of the Budhi Gandak.
