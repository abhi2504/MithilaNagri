**Jhanjharpur**, a subdivision headquarters on the Kamala in Madhubani district, is today best known as the gateway to **Mithila Haat**, the cultural theme-park at nearby Araria Sangram. A busy market and rail town, it anchors the eastern part of the district and serves a wide rural hinterland of villages.
