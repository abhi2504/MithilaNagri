**Simaria**, on the bank of the Ganga in Begusarai district, is honoured as the birthplace of **Ramdhari Singh 'Dinkar'** (1908–1974) — the *Rashtrakavi*, "national poet", whose thunderous verse (*Rashmirathi*, *Kurukshetra*) made him one of independent India's most quoted writers and a Jnanpith laureate.

The village is also a riverside *tirtha*: **Simaria Dham** hosts a great **Kalpvas** fair each year, when pilgrims camp on the Ganga for a month of austerity. Poetry and pilgrimage together give this Begusarai village, on the southern edge of the Mithila plain, a name far larger than its size.
