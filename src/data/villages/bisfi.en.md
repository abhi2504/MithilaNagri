**Bisfi**, in Madhubani district, is revered as the birthplace of **Vidyapati** (c. 1352–1448), the *Maithil Kavi Kokil* who turned Maithili into a literary language. A village of farmers and scholars, it keeps the poet's memory through the annual *Vidyapati Parva*, and stands as a quiet place of pilgrimage for lovers of Maithili letters.
