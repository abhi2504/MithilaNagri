**Mahishi**, west of Saharsa, is home to the **Ugratara Sthan** — the only Ugratara temple in Bihar and an ancient seat of tantric devotion. The village is also remembered as the home of the great philosopher **Mandan Mishra**, whose legendary debate with Adi Shankaracharya is one of the celebrated episodes of Indian thought.
