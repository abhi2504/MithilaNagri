**Benipatti** is a block town in the north-west of Madhubani district, close to the **Uchhaith Bhagwati** temple where the poet Kalidasa is said to have been blessed. Set among the rivers of the upper Mithila plain near the Nepal border, it is a typical market town serving its surrounding farming villages.
