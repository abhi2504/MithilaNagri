**Pusa**, in Samastipur district, holds a singular place in Indian science: it was here that the **Imperial Agricultural Research Institute** was founded in 1905 (later moving to Delhi as IARI, which still carries the "Pusa" name). Today it is home to the **Dr. Rajendra Prasad Central Agricultural University**.

In a region whose life has always been bound to its fields and rivers, Pusa stands for Mithila's contribution to the science of the soil.
