**Pindaruch**, in Darbhanga district, is remembered as the birthplace (1831) of the poet **Chanda Jha** — *Kavi Chandra* — who gave Mithila its first **Ramayana in Maithili** and did more than anyone since Vidyapati to make the language a vehicle of serious literature.

Like Bisfi for Vidyapati, Pindaruch is one of those modest Maithil villages whose name is carried far beyond its fields by the work of a single great writer born within it.
