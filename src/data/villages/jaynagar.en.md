**Jaynagar**, on the India–Nepal border in Madhubani district, is the railhead from which the cross-border line runs to **Janakpur** — the revived rail link that carries pilgrims to Sita's city. A lively trading town, it is one of the main crossing points between the Indian and Nepali halves of Mithila.
