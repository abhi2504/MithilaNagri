**Jitwarpur**, near Madhubani town, is the most famous of the painting villages — recognised as one of Bihar's first craft villages. Of its roughly four hundred households, hundreds paint in the vivid, colour-filled **Bharni** style. It was home to legends such as **Sita Devi** and **Baua Devi**, whose work carried Mithila painting from village walls to museums across the world.
