**Phulhar**, near the Nepal border, is woven into the Ramayana: here, at the **Girija Sthan** temple of the goddess Bhagwati, Sita is said to have come to worship and to have first set eyes on Rama as he gathered flowers in the garden of Pushpavatika. A stop on the Mithila Madhya Parikrama, the village draws pilgrims retracing the geography of Sita's story.
