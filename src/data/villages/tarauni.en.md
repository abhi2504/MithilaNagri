**Tarauni** is a farming village in the **Benipur** block of **Darbhanga** district, Bihar, in the heart of Mithila. It is the family village of **Vaidyanath Mishra (1911–1998)**, the bilingual Hindi and Maithili poet known the world over as **Nagarjun** — the *Janakavi*, or "People's Poet." He began in the early 1930s writing Maithili verse under the name *Yatri*, and went on to become one of modern India's most fearless political poets.

Though Nagarjun was born at his mother's village of Satlakha (in present-day Madhubani district), Tarauni was his paternal home and the place most closely identified with him; he lived his later years in the Darbhanga region and died nearby in 1998. The **2011 Census** recorded the village at about **2,300** people across roughly 490 households, with its own gram panchayat.

The everyday language is **Maithili**, and the village is cherished by admirers of Nagarjun as a place of literary pilgrimage. Beyond its association with the poet, it remains a typical agrarian settlement of the Darbhanga plain.
