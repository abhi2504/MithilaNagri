**Madhwapur**, the northernmost block of Madhubani district, sits right on the **India–Nepal border**, facing the Nepali town of **Matihani** and the road on to **Janakpur**. For pilgrims and traders alike it is one of the everyday gateways between the Indian and Nepali halves of Mithila — a place where the same Maithili is spoken and the same festivals kept on either side of the boundary pillar.

Its position on the route to Sita's city gives it a quiet importance during **Vivaha Panchami** and the **Parikrama**, when the flow of devotees between the two countries swells.
