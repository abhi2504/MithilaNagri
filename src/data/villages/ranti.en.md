**Ranti** is the counterpart to Jitwarpur — the village famed for the delicate, line-built **Katchni** style of Madhubani painting. It produced master artists including **Mahasundari Devi**, **Karpoori Devi** and **Godavari Dutta**, and trained later Padma Shri honourees such as Dulari Devi. Where Jitwarpur dazzles with colour, Ranti draws the eye in close with shimmering networks of line.
