**Madhepura**, a district headquarters in the Kosi region, is known for two very different things. Just beside it stands **Singheshwar Sthan**, one of the most revered Shiva shrines of eastern Mithila, thronged in the month of Shravan. And in modern times the district became a byword for the assertive backward-caste politics of the Kosi belt — the home district of **B. P. Mandal**, whose Mandal Commission reshaped Indian public life.

A great electric-locomotive factory now sits here too, so that Madhepura joins old pilgrimage, fierce politics and new industry in one Kosi-side town.
