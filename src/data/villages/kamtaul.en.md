**Kamtaul** is the railway gateway to two of Darbhanga's most storied sites — **Ahilya Asthan**, where Rama is said to have freed Ahalya from her curse, and the nearby **Gautam Asthan**. For pilgrims tracing the Ramayana through Mithila, the village is a natural point of arrival.
